Notes

  • Chip temperature was not recorded. macOS shares no chip temperature without an extra tool. macOS logged no heat or slowdown warnings during the run. Fan use during your rests was not measured, so no adjustment was made.
  • Models ran in a fixed mode with no randomness, so repeat runs match.
  • Words per second equals tokens per second times 0.75. Treat words as an estimate.
  • moondream reads images. Its answers ran one to six words, so no speed or long log time is shown.
  • llama3:latest and llama3:8b have the same size and settings and scored the same. Treat them as one model.
